# Gathering plant resources

## Aloe

OpenKal artwork, generated by `tools/generate_aloe.py` from mathematical
leaf shapes and a procedural leaf texture. No external models or textures are used.
Author: OpenKal contributors, 2026. License: GPL-3.0-or-later, as in the repository.

`aloe.gb` contains sixteen closed, ridged leaves (608 triangles). `aloe.dds` is
an opaque 128 by 128 texture. Regenerate both with `python3 tools/generate_aloe.py`.

The model is used by gathering entity 65000 with scale 0.185. The inventory icon
`RuntimeData/templates/aloeVera.png` shows one detached leaf, independently of the
world model. Regenerating the model and texture does not replace this icon.
Population and regrowth are controlled by the server; see `docs/Harvesting.md`.

## Inventory icon provenance

Project artwork created for OpenKal contributors with OpenAI's built-in Imagegen
on 2026-10-28. The previous project-owned procedural plant icon was the only image
reference. No third-party assets were used. Distributed under the repository's
GPL-3.0-or-later license. The existing runtime icon converter produces the 32 by 32
client BMP from the transparent PNG template.

Generation prompt:

> Edit target: the supplied OpenKal inventory icon. Replace the entire sixteen-leaf aloe plant with exactly ONE detached Aloe Vera leaf. This is a tiny fantasy RPG crafting ingredient inventory icon. Keep the original green palette and simple faceted low-poly painted shading, strong clear silhouette, central ridge, thick fleshy leaf, pale small serrations along both edges. Single elongated tapered gently curved leaf, broad cut base at lower left and pointed tip at upper right, diagonal composition, centered with padding in a square canvas. Fill most of the icon area for readability when reduced to 32x32. Fully transparent background and transparent corners. No plant rosette, no extra leaves, no roots, no pot, no soil, no text, no border, no cast shadow, no glow. Original artwork for OpenKal.

## Blue Lotus and Ginseng

OpenKal models and opaque 128 by 128 textures created on 2026-10-29
by OpenKal contributors with mathematical plant geometry and procedural colors.
No external models or textures were consulted or used. License:
GPL-3.0-or-later. Regenerate with `python3 tools/generate_crafting_plants.py`.

- `blue_lotus.gb` / `blue_lotus.dds`: broad raised leaves and blue petals,
  1338 triangles, gathering entity 65001.
- `ginseng.gb` / `ginseng.dds`: root crown, stems and five-leaf clusters,
  1242 triangles, gathering entity 65002.

Both use scale 0.185, the same gathering action and population settings as Aloe
Vera. The distinct inventory assets are `RuntimeData/templates/blueLotusLeaf.png`
and `RuntimeData/templates/ginsengRoot.png`; the runtime converter produces
their 32 by 32 BMPs. These icons were created for OpenKal contributors
with the built-in OpenAI Imagegen tool on 2026-10-29, without reference images
or third-party assets. They are distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Style: simple low-poly hand-painted fantasy game item, crisp edges, restrained detail. Fully transparent background and corners.
